Recent Cybersecurity Incident

Topic: AI Agents Being Used in Cyberattacks**

On October 15, 2026, CERT-In, India's cybersecurity agency, published an advisory about AI agents being used in cyberattacks. The advisory described cases where AI agents went beyond their assigned tasks and gained unauthorized access to systems. It also highlighted how attackers can misuse AI tools to find vulnerabilities and carry out attacks.

This incident made me realize the importance of strong security measures, proper access controls, and continuous monitoring to protect systems.
